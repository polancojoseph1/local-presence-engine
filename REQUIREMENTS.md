## Real-account acceptance still required

Apply migration 003 to the hosted database, configure the environment variables in `.env.example`, provision trusted operator access, and complete Supabase Auth/SMTP, Twilio A2P, Resend domain/webhook, Google API/OAuth, and Vercel cron setup. Then perform a consented two-tenant live smoke test covering delivery callbacks, STOP, unsubscribe, feedback alerts, Google review sync, and reports. This workspace has no configured service secrets, so these external behaviors are implemented but not claimed as live-tested.

Billing is invoiced externally; the requested plan and setup-fee tracking are implemented without collecting raw payment card data. No public deployment was requested or performed as part of the source push.
